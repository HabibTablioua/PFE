\chapter*{Abstract}
\justifying

As part of our final year project, we developed a comprehensive platform for managing and simulating financial transactions based on the ISO 8583 standard, structured around a microservices architecture. This solution aims to provide a robust and flexible environment for simulating, monitoring, and analyzing the exchange of financial messages between payment terminals, ATMs, and banking systems.

Authentication and access security are ensured through the use of JWT tokens. Financial messages are automatically decoded and encoded according to industry standards, guaranteeing their compliance and integrity throughout the process. The platform centralizes incident management, allowing for efficient reporting, tracking, and resolution of any issues that may arise during exchanges. Users and administrators are notified in real time of important events thanks to an integrated notification system. Log monitoring provides complete visibility into system activity and facilitates troubleshooting in case of anomalies. A detailed transaction history allows users to view, filter, and analyze all simulated or processed operations, while request orchestration and centralized exchanges ensure the fluidity and consistency of all interactions. Dynamic service discovery further guarantees the resilience and scalability of the architecture.

The backend, developed in Java with Spring Boot and Spring Cloud, relies on MySQL for data persistence. The frontend, built with Angular, offers a modern and intuitive interface that enables users to configure messages, simulate transactions, view incidents, monitor logs, access transaction history, and receive notifications.

This project, carried out using an Agile Scrum approach and validated through rigorous testing, allowed us to master payment industry standards and design a high-performance distributed architecture that meets critical needs for testing, validation, and supervision of financial systems.

\noindent \textbf{Keywords:} Microservices, Java, Spring Boot, Spring Cloud, Angular, MySQL, ISO 8583, Authentication, Security, Monitoring, Notification, Incident Management, History, Agile Scrum.
